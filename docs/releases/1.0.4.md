## What changed

- The Lyrics view has a three-line stage for the previous, current and next synced line, with track details and LRCLIB attribution below. Home shows the current line, leaves the first second of a lookup blank, and gives clearer empty-state actions.
- The scrubber follows the cover colour for new installs and thickens on hover. Track titles hand off on a change, view switches ease in, and the keyboard focus ring follows the tabs. Reduced motion is respected.
- Nookisle always shows the island. The island on/off setting and former stock widget UI are removed. A stored `island` value in `settings.json` is ignored and omitted on the next settings save; other preferences are preserved.
- The listing and README have fresh island footage, a silent promo video and a new gallery.
- GitHub Actions dependencies were upgraded. A shared version guard and bump script support the documented branch and release workflow. The security policy explains vulnerability reporting.

Updates fast-forward only. Every binary is attested.

## Fixed

- Fixed the v1.0.3 lyric-loading failure reported across every track. A busy LRCLIB response is retried once; narrower lookups follow a real miss. Errors now say “Lyrics unavailable” rather than “No lyrics found”. A lookup normally sends one request and can send at most four, using the same metadata fields or fewer.
- Closing the island cancels pending lyrics work. Errors are not cached; a real not-found result lasts for the session, and Try again is available for an error.

## Known limitations

- Existing installs keep their stored scrubber colour, including white. To change it, choose **Progress colour → Album art** in Settings (`sliderColor: "albumArt"`). The new default reaches only installs that have never saved settings.
- The camera Mirror tile (`showMirror`, off by default for new installs) keeps its feed running while Home is open and costs about 6 to 8 CPU percentage points on the reference machine; the performance cohorts below were measured with `showMirror` off.
- At the largest tested theme font size (18), Home is tight at the top edge and the next lyric line may not fully fit in the Lyrics stage. Sizes 15 and 13 fit the tested Home layout.
- When the host does not expose its lock service to plugins, a playing island can keep its local spectrum capture running behind the lock screen. No audio leaves the process; `visualizer: false` turns it off.
- LRCLIB availability and matching still depend on the service. Lyrics remain opt-in; no search endpoint or broader artist metadata is sent.

## What was tested

The final local release gate passed **64 of 64 CTest suites**, including QML, helper, settings migration, installer, browser/native-host and lifecycle contracts. The source contract and version guard passed. The direct lifecycle gate reported zero failures, and the staged distribution assembled with all shipped relative links resolving and passed `omarchy plugin validate`. CI runs only 18 non-QML tests; local QML checks supply the UI evidence.

The CPU cohorts were measured on **4d74375**, before the always-on island change, with Mirror off: three alternating pairs per cohort, 30 seconds of warmup and 120 seconds of sampling. CPU values are whole-owned-workload deltas in percentage points against the historical native-widget baseline; compositor deltas are reported separately.

| Historical cohort | Owned CPU median [pair range], points | Compositor median, points | Owned PSS median [pair range], MiB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collapsed, playing | 1.89 [1.28–2.00] | 0.64 | 7.9 [6.4–14.9] |
| Expanded, playing | 0.75 [0.55–0.94] | 0.30 | 9.0 [5.2–9.2] |
| Hover cycle | 3.20 [2.66–5.95] | −0.59 | 16.5 [16.2–18.1] |

All three historical cohort medians passed their budgets. Collapsed playback has a thin margin; one hover pair reached 5.95 points under load. No pair was discarded. These results describe the sampled revision, not a new performance acceptance for the always-on island. The new head was verified by the full CTest gate, lifecycle suite and package validation, with **no new CPU cohort run**. New island-off/on comparisons are retired; there is no development bypass.

On **4d74375**, before the always-on island change, live summon, track skip, Home/Shelf/Lyrics switches, Escape, motion settings and synced lyrics were checked only on the maintainer’s own Omarchy display. Real LRCLIB checks on that same revision returned `ok 200` for a canonical track and for the no-album and stripped-title fallbacks, with `ok 404` for a deliberately wrong album. Font layouts were checked offscreen at sizes 18, 15 and 13. This does not establish coverage on other displays or GPUs.
